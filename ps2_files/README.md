# GameSpot PS2 artwork

The masthead, header backgrounds, page texture, Go button, footer logo and
`gs.css` were recovered through the Internet Archive replay of:

https://web.archive.org/web/20030602172538/http://www.gamespot.com/ps2/

Original asset locations:

- `http://image.com.com/gamespot/shared/pi2/`: `g1.jpg`, `g2_ps2.jpg`,
  `head_bg.jpg`, `head_bg1.jpg`, `top_bg.gif`
- `http://image.com.com/gamespot/shared/`: `gs_go.gif`, `gsbotnav.gif`
- `http://image.com.com/gamespot/shared/styles/gs.css`
- `http://www.gamespot.com/shared/pc_bg.gif`

Assets were requested using the `20030602172538id_` replay prefix; Wayback
may resolve individual assets to their nearest available capture.

`badges.json` maps review badge names to the existing badge directory's images,
original hover descriptions and link anchors. The two additional GIFs in this
folder were extracted from the directory's embedded images. Badge artwork is
from the later emblem era; it belongs to the reviews that awarded those badges,
while the page layout follows the 2003 PS2 design and the local GBC review table.

All artwork and descriptions remain the property of their respective owners.

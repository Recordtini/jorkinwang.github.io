GameSpot PS2 Game Emblems

Every badge from the mid-2007 emblem era, recovered from archived PlayStation 2 reviews. Each entry shows the emblem image as GameSpot shipped it, the badge title, the full hover text GameSpot attached to each emblem (hover over the image, just like the original), the source filename, and every game that earned it — expand the list to see them all. 53 emblems across 70 reviewed games.
